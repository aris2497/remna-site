Deadstock silk fabric

Silk is light, smooth and drapes beautifully: crepe de chine, satin, chiffon, organza and silk twill for blouses, dresses, slips and linings. Deadstock silk often comes from Italian and French fashion houses, at a fraction of the price of new cloth.
