Deadstock viscose fabric

Viscose (rayon) is made from wood pulp and drapes like silk at a lower price. Crepe, twill, satin and challis in viscose are popular for flowing dresses, blouses and wide trousers.

Right now Remna lists 3,189 viscose fabrics from 22 shops. Prices are typically €4.50 – €20.00 per metre (median €10.00).
