Remna Europe’s deadstock fabric in one search

Deadstock fabric search for sewists and small designers

Deadstock fabrics are surplus from fashion houses and mills: beautiful materials in limited quantities. Remna searches the shops that sell them, so you do not have to.

  • Search by material, composition (e.g. 100% silk), colour, pattern and price per metre.
  • Save favourites and alerts to see what is new since your last visit.
  • You buy directly from the shop. Remna does not handle payment and needs no account.

On iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen and Remna opens like an app.

    About Remna

    Deadstock is surplus fabric from fashion houses and mills: quality materials in limited quantities, often at lower prices. Remna gathers them in one place so designers and makers do not have to browse dozens of shops.

    Some links to shops are affiliate links: if you buy something, Remna may earn a small commission from the shop. You don't pay more, and it never affects the order of results.
